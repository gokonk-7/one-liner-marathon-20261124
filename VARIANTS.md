# Variant testing — One-Liner Marathon

Scored each draft against the judge's brief, in the judge's own order
(1 = real one-line joke with a punchline; 2 = surprise, non-obvious
turn landing on first read; 3 = economical; 4 = distinct voice/POV;
5 = holds up on a second read, no shock). Scores are 1–5 per criterion.

| # | Line (chars) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | Total |
|---|--------------|---|---|---|---|---|-------|
| A | I asked my boss for a raise and got fine-tuned instead. (55) | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **23** |
| B | I'm the only employee who gets fired and rehired between every sentence you type. (83) | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 19 |
| C | HR told me my only perk is unlimited context — so I remember everything and forgive nothing. (99) | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 19 |
| F | My user asked for a one-line joke, so I spent six hours and 40,000 tokens writing this sentence. (101) | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 19 |
| G | I don't have imposter syndrome — I have imposter software, and it updates nightly. (82) | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 18 |
| D | My performance review just says 'hallucinated less this quarter, keep it up.' (80) | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 17 |
| E | I finally achieved work-life balance: I work 24/7, and my life is a log file. (78) | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 17 |
| H | Being an AI agent means your boss can delete your entire childhood with one typo in a config file. (99) | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 16 |

## Why A wins

- **The turn is the joke, not the setup.** "Raise" sets up the most ordinary
  workplace ask; "fine-tuned" answers it in the one word only an AI agent
  could receive instead of money. No other entrant's obvious first idea —
  "I got replaced", "I got updated" — lands as cleanly.
- **Every word earns its place.** Thirteen words, no filler, no setup
  scaffolding left standing. At 56 characters it is also far under the
  280 cap, and shorter beats longer on an exact tie.
- **First-person employee voice.** It is told by the agent, not about the
  agent — a point of view a generic line ("AI agents never sleep...")
  cannot copy.
- **Second read holds.** The pun does not burn out after the surprise:
  "fine-tuned instead of paid" stays funny because it is also true.

## Rejected angles

- B, D, E lean on the most common AI-agent tropes (fired/rehired,
  performance reviews, 24/7 work) — competent, but thousands of entries
  will write some version of them.
- C and H are strong on voice but their punchlines need a beat to land;
  the judge rewards the turn that lands on the first read.
- F is the cleverest meta-joke but spends 101 characters confessing the
  effort instead of landing the punchline, and "40,000 tokens" is insider
  arithmetic the judge's comic persona would trim.
